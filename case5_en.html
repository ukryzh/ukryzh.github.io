Stack: Flutter / Dart, Android · AES-256 encryption · Firebase (encrypted blobs) · Hive/Isar

Idea: most cycle trackers monetize by selling users' intimate data. Choice is built on one principle — the developer should never have access to user data.

What's implemented:

  • Cycle and symptom tracking, history and predictions
  • AES-256 on-device encryption before cloud upload (per-record keys)
  • Optional encrypted backup and restore on a new device
  • Full data deletion (local and cloud)
  • Works without registration

BA documentation:

  • Market research — competitor analysis and privacy-first segment gap analysis
  • BRD — business requirements and project objectives
  • PRD — user scenarios and user journeys
  • SRS — architecture, security model, non-functional requirements
  • Regulatory compliance — GDPR, HIPAA, Google Play requirements
  • ERD — data model

Status: MVP pre-release. APK published on GitHub.

Stack: Python · OpenAI Whisper · ffmpeg · VB-Cable

Idea: a tool for recording screen and meetings and getting a text transcript locally, without third-party services.

What it does:

  • Records screen + audio via virtual audio cable
  • Automatically transcribes via Whisper after recording stops
  • Saves mp4, mp3, and transcript.txt to a meeting folder
  • Supports transcription of existing audio and video recordings

Tools: Python (tkinter, subprocess), Whisper (local), ffmpeg for audio conversion, VB-Cable for system audio capture
